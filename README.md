# grey-kalico

## Overview

This repository serves as a running record of my progress in IT and electronics. I am currently working toward the CompTIA A+ certification, with a focused interest in cybersecurity and network engineering. The work documented here spans lab setups, capture-the-flag challenges, embedded systems tinkering, and general notes from study sessions. Everything is written for my own reference, but made public in case it is useful to others on a similar path.

---

## Background

I am at the early stages of a self-directed technical education. My primary areas of study are hardware fundamentals, operating systems, and networking — the core subjects covered by the CompTIA A+ exam. Alongside formal study, I spend time on practical work: configuring virtual machines, poking at small security challenges, and building simple circuits with Arduino boards.

The long-term direction is toward cybersecurity operations and network infrastructure, though I am keeping that path open as I learn more about what each area actually involves day to day.

## What You Will Find Here

**Study Notes and Write-ups**
Summaries of concepts I have worked through, mostly from the CompTIA A+ syllabus. These are not polished guides — they are working documents that reflect where I am in the learning process.

**Virtual Machine Setups**
Documentation of lab environments I have built, including what I installed, how I configured it, and what I ran into along the way. Current setups include Windows and Linux virtual machines using VirtualBox.

**CTF Attempts**
Notes and partial write-ups from capture-the-flag challenges. I am a beginner in this area, so most of these will be straightforward problems with basic techniques. The goal is to build the habit of thinking methodically about security problems.

**Arduino Projects**
Small electronics and programming projects using Arduino. These range from basic input/output experiments to anything that seems interesting enough to wire up.
